Cyber App — Learn Cybersecurity by Playing

Cyber App is a gamified cybersecurity learning world for kids and teens (10–25). Create companies, defend them, launch raids, complete missions, and level up your skills inside a futuristic cyber world.

Get Started
Designed for learners — play ethically, learn to protect.

Core Game Modes

Hack & Run

Engage in ethical hacking missions: solve quizzes and technical challenges to breach a target's defenses. Targets are player-run companies that set up puzzles and traps for attackers.

Build Your Company

Design infrastructure, pick services, and harden systems. Make architecture choices that influence your company's attack surface and reputation.

Cyber Academy

Take courses on networking, cryptography, secure coding, and defense. Apply what you learn to build an almost unhackable company — if you do the work.

Hack & Run — How it Works

Players can create challenge rooms inside their companies: quizzes, CTF-style tasks, logic puzzles, or configuration challenges. Attackers attempt to break in by solving these puzzles under rules that encourage learning and fair play. Successful attacks earn loot, reputation, and new mission types.

Build Your Company

Choose services, deploy simulated servers, configure firewalls and monitoring, and buy security upgrades. Companies can hire advisors and set bounty-like challenge puzzles to test employees and the community.

Cyber Academy

Structured learning paths cover networking fundamentals, TCP/IP, authentication, cryptography basics, secure development, and attacker mindset. Lessons are hands-on and tied to in-game rewards so learning unlocks new gameplay features.

Gameplay at a Glance

  1. Create a company — design network, services and defenses.
  2. Join missions or set challenges for others in your company.
  3. Attempt Hack & Run raids by solving puzzles and real-world inspired tasks.
  4. Complete Academy lessons to unlock defensive tools and advanced missions.
